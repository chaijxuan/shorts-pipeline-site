Privacy policy: shorts-pipeline

Last updated: 8 November 2026

shorts-pipeline is a personal tool operated by the owner of a single YouTube channel. It accesses only that owner's own YouTube account, through Google OAuth and with the owner's consent.

What data is accessed

  • Channel and video information and analytics (views, watch time, retention, likes, shares, subscribers gained) for the owner's channel.
  • Permission to upload videos and thumbnails that the owner has approved.

How it is used

Data is used only to upload and schedule the owner's videos and to analyze their performance. It is stored privately by the owner and is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. This app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, and use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on this app's Google sign-in screen.
